FAQ

Before you message me.

What do I need to send you?

Just your raw footage. If you have a logo, brand colors or reference videos you love, send them too.

What's included?

Everything from raw to ready-to-post: cuts and pacing, hooks, captions, sound design, color, B-roll, and visual touches that explain and reinforce your message.

Can you match a style I like?

Absolutely. Send me references and I'll adapt that style to fit your brand and your voice.

Do you edit Arabic and English content?

Yes, both, with the same care, from captions to pacing.

What about pricing, timeline and revisions?

Every project is different. We agree on all of it together before we start, based on your goals and how much content you need.
